自點 ZiTap 隱私權政策

開發者： Je Lab
適用版本： Google Play 版 (com.jelab.zitap)
生效日期： 2026年11月13日

Je Lab 重視您的隱私。本政策說明「自點 ZiTap」(以下稱本 App) 如何存取、使用、保存及分享資料，以及您可以如何管理這些資料。

1. 無障礙服務與畫面操作

本 App 需經您在 Android 設定中啟用無障礙服務，才能錄製及執行點擊、滑動，並依您設定的腳本讀取畫面文字、尋找輸入框、輸入文字或送出內容。

視您使用的功能而定，本 App 可能存取目前畫面的文字、輸入框提示、元件資訊及位置。這些內容可能包含其他 App 或網頁顯示的個人或敏感資訊。

上述資料用於執行您設定的操作及條件判斷，不會作為廣告內容或廣告個人化的輸入，也不會由本 App 傳送至 Je Lab 的伺服器。

您可隨時在系統設定中停用無障礙服務，或透過懸浮控制停止腳本。

本 App 不會自動破解驗證碼。「驗證碼處理」僅在偵測到您設定的提示文字時暫停腳本，供您自行完成驗證。

2. 畫面截圖、OCR 與圖片比對

使用圖片文字定位或等待圖片文字時，本 App 透過 Android 無障礙截圖功能暫時取得畫面影像，並使用 Google ML Kit 在裝置上辨識文字。

使用圖片定位點擊或等待指定圖片時，本 App 同樣暫時取得畫面影像，並在裝置上與您選取的目標圖案比對。用於辨識或比對的畫面只在處理期間暫存於記憶體。您確認框選的目標圖案會隨腳本保存在裝置上。系統禁止截圖時，相關功能可能無法執行。

本 App 不會將畫面截圖、辨識出的文字或目標圖案上傳至 Je Lab，或提供給 Google 作為辨識、分析或廣告資料。

ML Kit 仍可能向 Google 傳送裝置及 App 資訊、安裝識別資訊、效能、API 設定及錯誤等診斷與使用統計資料。這些技術資料與用於辨識的畫面影像及文字內容不同。

3. 本機保存的資料

本 App 會在 App 專用儲存空間保存您建立的腳本及設定，包括：

  • 腳本名稱與動作順序。
  • 點擊位置、滑動路徑、間隔及重複次數。
  • 執行時間、搜尋文字、輸入內容及驗證碼提示詞。

這些資料不會由本 App 自動同步至 Je Lab 的伺服器。您可手動匯出腳本至您選擇的位置。匯出檔可能包含腳本設定、輸入文字及目標圖案。若您選擇雲端儲存位置或分享檔案，接收方將依其服務政策或自身方式處理該副本。

若您將個人或敏感資料填入腳本的輸入內容，該內容也會隨腳本保存在裝置上。執行輸入或送出動作時，內容會依您的設定交給目標 App 或網頁，其後續蒐集、傳送與使用方式由該服務的隱私權政策規範。

4. 廣告與隱私選擇

未解鎖 Advanced 時，本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能蒐集並分享以下資料，以提供廣告、分析成效及防止詐欺：

  • IP 位址及由 IP 推估的概略位置。
  • 裝置或帳戶相關識別碼，例如廣告 ID 與 App set ID。
  • App／廣告互動及診斷資訊。

廣告資料的處理取決於適用地區、您的選擇、裝置設定及 Google 服務設定。本 App 使用 Google 的隱私同意工具，在適用情況下顯示同意視窗與廣告隱私選項。您亦可透過 Android 設定管理廣告 ID 與相關隱私選項。

解鎖 Advanced 後，本 App 不再載入或顯示廣告；Google Analytics for Firebase 仍可能蒐集使用情形。解鎖不會自動刪除 Google 先前已處理的資料。

5. 付費購買與恢復購買

Advanced 由 Google Play 提供一次性應用程式內購買，用於移除廣告並解鎖進階功能。

付款與帳務由 Google Play 處理；本 App 不會取得您的完整信用卡號或付款密碼。

本 App 會透過 Google Play Billing 查詢、驗證及確認購買，並在裝置上保存購買憑證、簽章及解鎖狀態等必要資訊，以提供功能解鎖、離線使用與恢復購買。購買資訊可能包含商品 ID、訂單或購買權杖、購買時間及狀態等。

本 App 目前不將這些憑證傳送至 Je Lab 自建的驗證伺服器。

本 App 不要求您建立 Je Lab 帳號；購買與恢復購買會使用 Google Play 管理的帳戶及交易紀錄。

6. 第三方服務與資料分享

本 App 使用 Google AdMob、Google 隱私同意工具、Google ML Kit、Google Play Billing 及 Google Analytics for Firebase。

Google 會依其隱私權政策與各服務規定處理必要資料，資料可能在您所在國家或地區以外處理。Je Lab 可能透過相關服務後台查看廣告成效或交易等報表；本 App 不會將您的腳本、畫面截圖或辨識文字提供作為廣告資料。

本 App 使用 Google Analytics for Firebase 瞭解 ZiTap 的使用情形及購買功能的使用情形。Google 可能接收首次開啟、工作階段、App 畫面瀏覽及應用程式內購買等事件，以及 App 執行個體 ID、可用的廣告 ID、裝置與 App 資訊、由 IP 位址推估的概略位置，以及購買事件的商品識別資訊與價格。Je Lab 可查看相關分析報表。腳本、截圖、目標圖案及 OCR 結果不會作為 Analytics 事件資料傳送。

本 App 的 Analytics 資源未啟用 Google 信號，且未連結 Google Ads、AdMob、BigQuery 或 Google Play。Analytics 帳戶關閉了「Google 產品和服務」的額外資料共用選項，並啟用了匯總且去識別化資料的模型與業務洞察、必要時供 Google 技術支援存取，以及使用 Analytics 帳戶資訊提供業務建議。Google 仍會處理資料以提供、維護及保護 Analytics 服務。AdMob 的廣告資料處理見第 4 節。

詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。

若您透過 Email 聯絡我們，我們會使用您提供的 Email、訊息及附件處理詢問或支援需求。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼或完整付款資訊。

7. 保存、刪除與資料控制

  • 您可在 App 內刪除腳本。刪除後，該腳本不再保留於目前的腳本清單中。
  • 清除 App 儲存資料或解除安裝，可移除 App 專用空間中的本機腳本、設定及購買快取；這不會取消已完成的購買，也不會刪除 Google 保留的交易紀錄。
  • 辨識及比對用的暫存畫面會在處理後釋放。您確認儲存的目標圖案隨腳本保存。刪除 App 內的腳本、清除 App 資料或解除安裝，都不會一併刪除您已匯出或分享的副本，請在各儲存位置另行管理。
  • Google Analytics 目前將一般事件資料的保留期限設為 2 個月，使用者層級資料的保留期限設為 14 個月。新的使用者活動會重設該使用者資料的保留期限。Google 會定期自動刪除到期資料。標準匯總報表不受這些期限限制。清除 App 資料或解除安裝不會自動刪除 Google 已收到的分析資料。詳情請參閱 Google Analytics 資料保留說明。
  • Google 處理的廣告、診斷及交易資料，依 Google 的保存政策與法律義務保留；清除本 App 資料不會自動刪除這些資料。
  • 若您寄送支援訊息，我們僅在處理詢問、後續追蹤及履行必要法律義務所需期間保存。您可透過下列 Email 就我們直接收到的支援資料提出查詢、更正或刪除請求；依法必須保存的資料除外。

8. 資料安全

本機資料使用 Android 提供的 App 專用儲存空間保護；本 App 未另外為腳本內容提供專屬加密或密碼鎖。

Google SDK 傳送相關資料時使用其提供的安全傳輸機制。請妥善保護裝置及 Google 帳戶；任何儲存或傳輸方式都無法保證絕對安全。

ZiTap Privacy Policy

Developer: Je Lab
Applies to: Google Play version (com.jelab.zitap)
Effective date: November 13, 2026

Je Lab values your privacy. This policy explains how ZiTap (the “App”) accesses, uses, stores and shares data, and how you can manage that data.

1. Accessibility service and screen interaction

You must enable the App’s accessibility service in Android settings to record and perform taps and swipes, and to read screen text, locate input fields, enter text or submit content according to your scripts.

Depending on the features you use, the App may access text, input hints, interface element information and positions on the current screen. This may include personal or sensitive information displayed by other apps or websites.

This information is used to perform your configured actions and evaluate conditions. It is not provided as input for ad content or ad personalization, and the App does not send it to Je Lab’s servers. You can disable the accessibility service in system settings at any time or stop a script using the floating controls.

The App does not automatically solve CAPTCHAs. CAPTCHA handling only pauses a script when your configured prompt text is detected so that you can complete verification yourself.

2. Screen capture, OCR and image matching

When you use image text tapping or wait for image text, the App temporarily captures the screen through Android’s accessibility screenshot feature and uses Google ML Kit to recognize text on your device.

When you tap a matching image or wait for a specified image, the App also captures the screen temporarily and compares it with your selected target image on your device. Screen images used for recognition or matching are held in memory during processing. The target image you select and confirm is stored on your device with the script. These features may be unavailable where Android blocks screen capture.

The App does not upload screenshots, recognized text or target images to Je Lab or provide them to Google for recognition, analytics or advertising.

ML Kit may still send device and app information, installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration and errors to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. This technical data is distinct from the screen images and text being recognized.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores your scripts and settings in app-specific storage, including:

  • Script names and action order.
  • Tap positions, swipe paths, intervals and repeat counts.
  • Execution schedules, search text, input content and CAPTCHA prompt keywords.

The App does not automatically synchronize this data to Je Lab’s servers. You can manually export scripts to a location you choose. Exports may contain script settings, input text and target images. If you choose a cloud storage location or share an export, the recipient handles that copy according to its own policies or practices.

If you include personal or sensitive information in a script’s input content, that information is also stored with the script on your device. When an input or submit action runs, the content is passed to the target app or website according to your settings. That service’s privacy policy governs its subsequent collection, transmission and use.

4. Ads and privacy choices

Before Advanced is unlocked, the App uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the following data for ad delivery, performance measurement and fraud prevention:

  • IP addresses and approximate location inferred from IP addresses.
  • Device or account-related identifiers, such as advertising IDs and app set IDs.
  • App/ad interactions and diagnostic information.

Ad data processing depends on your region, choices, device settings and Google service settings. The App uses Google’s consent tools to display consent forms and ad privacy options where applicable. You can also manage your advertising ID and related privacy options in Android settings.

After Advanced is unlocked, the App stops loading and displaying ads. Google Analytics for Firebase may continue collecting usage data. Unlocking Advanced does not automatically delete data previously processed by Google.

5. Purchases and restoring purchases

Advanced is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play that removes ads and unlocks advanced features. Google Play handles payment and billing. The App does not receive your full credit card number or payment password.

The App uses Google Play Billing to query, verify and acknowledge purchases. It stores necessary purchase receipts, signatures and unlock status on your device to provide access, offline use and purchase restoration. Purchase information may include product IDs, order IDs or purchase tokens, purchase times and status.

The App currently does not send these receipts to a verification server operated by Je Lab.

The App does not require a Je Lab account. Purchases and purchase restoration use account and transaction records managed by Google Play.

6. Third-party services and data sharing

The App uses Google AdMob, Google’s consent tools, Google ML Kit, Google Play Billing and Google Analytics for Firebase.

Google processes necessary data under its privacy policy and the terms of each service. Data may be processed outside your country or region. Je Lab may view ad performance or transaction reports through these services. The App does not provide your scripts, screenshots or recognized text as advertising data.

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how ZiTap and its purchase features are used. Google may receive events such as first opens, sessions, app screen views and in-app purchases, along with app-instance IDs, available advertising IDs, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP addresses, and product identifiers and prices for purchase events. Je Lab can view related analytics reports. Scripts, screenshots, target images and OCR results are excluded from Analytics event data.

Google Signals is disabled for the App’s Analytics property, and the property has no links to Google Ads, AdMob, BigQuery or Google Play. The Analytics account has the optional Google products and services sharing setting turned off. It allows aggregated and de-identified data for modeling and business insights, access by Google technical support when needed, and use of Analytics account information for business recommendations. Google still processes data to provide, maintain and protect Analytics. Section 4 describes AdMob’s handling of advertising data.

For details, see the Google Privacy Policy.

If you contact us by email, we use the email address, messages and attachments you provide to respond to your inquiry or support request. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or full payment details.

7. Retention, deletion and your controls

  • You can delete scripts in the App. Deleted scripts are removed from the current script list.
  • Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App removes local scripts, settings and purchase caches from app-specific storage. This does not cancel completed purchases or delete transaction records retained by Google.
  • Temporary screen images used for recognition and matching are released after processing. Target images you confirm are kept with their scripts. Deleting scripts in the App, clearing app storage or uninstalling the App leaves exported or shared copies in their respective locations, where you can manage them separately.
  • Google Analytics is currently configured to retain general event data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. New user activity resets the retention period for that user’s data. Google periodically deletes expired data. Standard aggregated reports are outside these retention limits. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App does not automatically delete analytics data already received by Google. See Google Analytics data retention.
  • Google retains ad, diagnostic and transaction data under its retention policies and legal obligations. Clearing the App’s data does not automatically delete that data.
  • If you send support messages, we retain them only as needed to handle your request, follow up and meet necessary legal obligations. You may contact the email address below to request access, correction or deletion of support data we received directly, except where retention is legally required.

8. Data security

Local data is protected by Android’s app-specific storage. The App does not provide additional dedicated encryption or a password lock for script content.

Google SDKs use their provided secure transmission mechanisms for related data. Please protect your device and Google account. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.
